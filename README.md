# From Customer Voice to Delivery Execution

**Finexa · Merchant Experience Product Group · Payments Pilot**
12-Week Hybrid Agentic System Pilot

AIxPM capstone project, THRIVE program (Kiron Open Higher Education), 2026. Winner of the capstone competition.

Finexa's product teams collect merchant feedback from many channels but turn it into delivery decisions by hand. This pilot designs an AI-augmented feedback-to-delivery pipeline for the Payments domain. Feedback is ingested, de-identified and scored, then passes three human-oversight gates before it reaches Jira. A monitoring portal tracks both the AI system and the business outcomes.
