# From Customer Voice to Delivery Execution

**Finexa · Merchant Experience Product Group · Payments Pilot**
12-Week Hybrid Agentic System Pilot

THRIVE AI Project Management program (Kiron Open Higher Education), 2026. Business case provided by Mollie.

## Starting point

Finexa is a fintech whose Payments product group was overwhelmed by customer feedback:

- **Six disconnected channels:** interviews, support tickets, sales & account notes, ops escalations, Jira requests and compliance & risk feedback.
- **Manual synthesis:** PMs read and consolidated feedback by hand, one item at a time.
- **No traceability:** backlog items could not be traced back to verified customer needs.
- **Prioritization by anecdote**, not by the evidence that existed.
- **Incomplete tickets:** engineering regularly received tickets with missing context, which caused rework.

Leadership asked for a controlled pilot to move to evidence-based delivery planning, without removing PM authority or compromising security and compliance.

## What I did

1. **Scoping:** defined a 12-week pilot in the Payments domain, with clear in-scope (V1) and deferred (V2+) capabilities, built on Finexa's existing AI platform and BigQuery as the single source of truth.
2. **Stakeholder analysis:** mapped the Head of Product, Senior PM, Engineering Lead, Risk & Compliance and Support Ops by influence and interest, with a RACI matrix and each stakeholder's concerns designed into the system.
3. **System design:** a Hybrid Agentic System with four AI commands (`/process-feedback`, `/log-feedback`, `/prioritize-opportunities`, `/draft-user-story`) plus `/ask-data` for natural-language analytics.
4. **Human oversight:** three mandatory gates. Gate 1 confirms the reading and grouping and is the only gate designed to graduate to spot-checks; Gate 2 approves opportunities for drafting; Gate 3 approves the Jira draft and complexity.
5. **Deterministic guardrails:** priority is a transparent weighted formula over five criteria, never generated by the model; Jira credentials live only in a deterministic wrapper that fires after Gate 3.
6. **Data model:** an 11-table BigQuery schema covering raw feedback, insights, opportunities, gate decisions, Jira sync and AI run metrics.
7. **Compliance by design:** Google Cloud DLP de-identifies all feedback before it reaches the model; role-only logging for the Works Council (BetrVG §87); GDPR review; classified as a low-risk internal tool under the EU AI Act; full audit trail inherited from BigQuery.
8. **Risk management:** risk register with severity scoring, top-5 risks, mitigations and owners, dependencies and single points of failure, plus the executive decisions that block live data.
9. **Monitoring plan:** baseline capture in weeks 1–4, three analytics layers (product, process, AI operations), escalation triggers and a Proceed / Iterate / Stop decision in week 12.

## Outcome

- **Winner of the THRIVE capstone competition** among 150 projects.
- **Measurable targets:** ≥85% tag alignment, >80% RAG hit rate, >85% Gate 3 acceptance and >50% less time spent on feedback management.
- **A complete delivery package:** Project Brief, Stakeholder Map & RACI, Risk Mitigation Analysis and Monitoring Plan, plus an interactive prototype of the pipeline, the gates and the monitoring portal.
